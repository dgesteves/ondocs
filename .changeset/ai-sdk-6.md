ondocs works with AI SDK 6 as well as 7: the `ai` peer range is now `^6.0.16 || ^7.0.95`, and `@ai-sdk/openai`'s `^3.0.0 || ^4.0.0`, so a project on AI SDK 6 can add ondocs without moving to 7. On AI SDK 6, the endpoint streams answers through the result's own `toUIMessageStream()`, which AI SDK 6 has in place of AI SDK 7's helper, and passes the instructions, the end-of-answer callback and `generation.telemetry` by the names AI SDK 6 reads; `ondocs/mock` and Workers AI embeddings use model classes both read. AI SDK 6 has no timeout for the model's first word, so there an answer's default timeout is the 90 seconds for the whole answer. On AI SDK 7 the endpoint makes the same calls as before, but as it now looks the helper up on `ai` rather than importing it by name, a bundler keeps all of `ai` in the endpoint's bundle: about 53 KB more gzipped, from 185 KB to 238 KB, for `createAskHandler` bundled with esbuild. ondocs now needs Node.js 22.13 or later, up from 22.12, where loading an ES module with `require()` prints an experimental warning and one that fails to load ends the process. [Compatibility](https://ondocs.vercel.app/docs/getting-started#compatibility) lists what CI runs.
