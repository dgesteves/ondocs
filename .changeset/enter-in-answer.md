The keyboard works on the dialog's answer. Enter follows a citation, a source or a link in the answer, and presses the thumbs, "New question", "Retry" and "Stop", where before the command palette around them cancelled it, so a link could not be followed from the keyboard at all. The arrow keys, Home and End are left to scroll the answer. Escape still closes the dialog, and the keys in the input are unchanged.
